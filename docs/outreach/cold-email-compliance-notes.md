# Cold Email Compliance Notes

This document is implementation guidance, not legal advice.

Cold investor outreach drafts should:

- Use a truthful subject line.
- Clearly identify the sender.
- Explain why the recipient may be relevant.
- Avoid false relationships, fake warm intros, and exaggerated urgency.
- Include an opt-out line.
- Avoid hidden tracking pixels.

Scaled outbound campaigns should be reviewed by counsel or a qualified compliance owner before sending through any future connector.
