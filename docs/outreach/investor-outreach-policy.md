# Investor Outreach Policy

Investor outreach is a draft-and-review workflow.

## Allowed

- Source-backed investor lead discovery from public web research.
- Source-backed fit scoring.
- Draft outreach email generation.
- Action proposal creation for human review.
- Shortlist, dismiss, approve, or reject review states.

## Disabled

- Autonomous email sending.
- Hidden tracking pixels.
- Deceptive subjects.
- Fabricated warm intros.
- Contact-form submission.
- Browser login or cookie access.
- Purchased-list ingestion without future compliance review.
- Gmail send/write scopes.

## Required Checks

- Every investor lead must have at least one stored source.
- Drafts must include an opt-out line for cold outreach.
- Drafts must identify the sender honestly.
- Drafts must not claim traction, relationships, thesis, or partner interest unless source-backed or user-provided.
