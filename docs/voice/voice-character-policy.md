# Voice Character Policy

NearMind supports five built-in assistant voice characters:

- Calm Guide: default general assistant voice.
- Professional: business-like voice for work and investor contexts.
- Warm Support: softer voice for stressful moments, without therapeutic claims.
- Focus Whisper: short, low-distraction cues for Whisper Copilot.
- Briefing Voice: crisp summaries and daily brief output.

These are product presets, not cloned voices.

Rules:

- No voice sample upload.
- No voice cloning.
- No celebrity or public-person imitation.
- No imitation of friends, investors, bosses, partners, or the user.
- No synthetic voice for deception.
- No long screen-only reports spoken aloud.
- Focus Whisper is restricted to short cues.
