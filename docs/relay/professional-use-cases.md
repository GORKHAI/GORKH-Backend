# Professional Use Cases

Relay v0 supports private, reviewable drafts for:

- Asking whether someone is available for a meeting or investor call.
- Asking an investor whether they want a short text brief.
- Asking a candidate whether they are open to a role.
- Asking a professional contact about collaboration.
- Asking a known contact for a follow-up.
- Preparing a room invite request without sending a guest link externally.

Relay v0 does not support:

- Public feeds.
- Likes, comments, follows, or public discovery.
- Broadcasts to all contacts or all investors.
- Company manager surveillance.
- External A2A federation.
- Arbitrary MCP tool calls.
- Automatic sharing of decks, calendar, email, or memory.

Company workspace agents and team request routing are future features.
